Relay — AI Project Instruction

Purpose

Relay is an AI-native Android application that helps people remember how relationships began, not just who someone is.

Unlike traditional contact apps or social networks, Relay builds a Personal Relationship Graph. Every meaningful encounter becomes structured knowledge that AI can understand, search, and explain years later.

Product Vision

«Connect people with NFC. Preserve the story with AI.»

Relay treats relationships as a graph rather than a list of contacts. The goal is to create a second memory for human connections while keeping the user in complete control of their data.

Core Principles

- NFC is the entry point. New relationships begin with a simple NFC tap.
- Events are evidence. Time, location, nearby people, and shared files become factual records of a meeting.
- AI suggests, never decides. AI may propose relationships such as "Father's friend" or "School teacher," but the user always confirms them.
- Privacy first. Relationship data belongs to the user and is stored locally by default.

Graph Model

Nodes

- Person — people and contacts
- Event — meetings and encounters
- Place — locations where events occurred
- File — documents, photos, or business cards exchanged

Edges

Edges represent relationships and contextual connections.

Examples:

- FRIEND
- FAMILY
- TEACHER
- COLLEAGUE
- MET_AT
- SHARED_FILE

Edges may include metadata such as confidence score, creation time, and supporting evidence.

AI Responsibilities

The AI should:

1. Record factual meeting context.
2. Analyze patterns across events.
3. Suggest likely relationship labels.
4. Explain the origin of a relationship in natural language.
5. Never fabricate facts or overwrite confirmed relationships.

MVP Scope

1. NFC contact exchange
2. Automatic event creation (time + place)
3. AI relationship suggestion
4. Natural-language relationship search

Long-Term Direction

Relay is not a social media platform.

It is a personal relationship operating system where users can ask:

- "How did I meet this person?"
- "Who was with me when we first met?"
- "Find everyone I met at the Seoul conference."

The graph exists to help people remember their real-world connections with trustworthy, explainable AI.
